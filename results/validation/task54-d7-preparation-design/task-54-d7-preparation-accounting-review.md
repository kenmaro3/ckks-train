## Important I1 — compatibility handoff can outlive its accounting owner; the proposed lifetime factoring also narrows the existing wrapper contract

**Evidence:** proposal lines 18–37 and 89; `crates/ckks-train-bench/src/study_client.rs:153–159,411–436`; `crates/ckks-train-bench/src/study_client/blocks.rs:48–53`; ratified `docs/study-execution-design.md:119` and accounting/source notes M1.

The proposed canonical owner correctly carries accounting alongside checked geometry. However, the stated compatibility rule only keeps accounting alive through the existing factory invocation. One current invocation is `prepare_initial`, which passes `Ok` as the factory, receives the owned `CheckedPreparation`, then constructs arrays and calls `encrypt_initial` afterward (`study_client.rs:419–436`). Delegating that wrapper to the proposed retained owner and passing only its `checked` field into `Ok` drops accounting while checked geometry remains live. This is a concrete existing path, not a hypothetical external callback. Keeping the callback private does not prevent its result from owning the checked object. The source package therefore has not yet specified a complete retention/consumption boundary for its compatibility paths.

There is also a type-level mismatch in the same handoff. Existing wrappers take independently short-lived `PreparationInput<'_>` and `PlanningParameters<'a>`, and promise `CheckedPreparation<'a>` to the callback. The proposed `CanonicalPreparationInput<'a>` ties materialized/case/request borrows to the parameter lifetime. An unchanged generic wrapper cannot delegate to it and deliver the existing callback argument for arbitrary independent input/parameter lifetimes: shortening the parameter borrow to the input lifetime cannot subsequently lengthen the returned checked preparation. This is a source-level type analysis, not a compiler result.

**Required before source release:** specify a concrete consuming handoff for the existing `prepare_initial` caller, keeping the same accounting owner through the checked object's final consumption (for example, perform that existing encryption work inside the wrapper callback), and audit the other wrapper results for escaped checked ownership. No new crypto execution is authorized by this correction. Separate input-owner and parameter-owner lifetimes in the canonical input/result (`RetainedPreparation<'input, CheckedPreparation<'params>>`, and the block equivalent), or explicitly describe a compatible wrapper/internal factoring that preserves the current lifetime independence. Do not solve the escape by resetting the receipt, cloning/rebuilding geometry, or claiming the dropped accounting still owns the retained payload. Keep canonical phase2 output nonauthorizing and disconnected from production start.

## Minor M1 — finish the binding workspace inventory at its actual helper boundaries

**Evidence:** proposal lines 78–82,106,111; `study_admission.rs:891–905,1990–1994`; `crates/ckks-train-ckks/src/params.rs:51–57`; `study_reference.rs:218–220,588–597,633–651`; `crates/ckks-train-bench/src/data.rs:411–426`.

The proposed real counting/output hook is the right approach, but its listed binding buffers are not yet a complete call-site inventory. `secure_parameters` allocates preset bit vectors, including temporary extension vectors; final `validate_plan_binding` additionally hashes the retained plan after `canonical_binding` returns. Identity source sorting currently uses `sort_by`, so counting only pointer-vector elements must not silently assume that the sorting algorithm requires no scratch allocation. Distinguish logical application payload from any deliberately excluded library scratch rather than presenting the latter as zero. The two identity outputs also retain four 64-byte digest strings in total, including the cloned contract digest and materialized digest.

Carry these into the source allocation table: identify their live intervals, include them in the appropriate maximum or explicitly remove the allocations with byte-preserving narrow changes, and show why any excluded internal scratch is outside the stated requested-payload convention. The successful size pass must not itself build vectors, hashes, or sorted pointer buffers before reservation. This note does not establish that the eventual peak is too small; early preset temporaries may be dominated by a later peak. It requires that the claimed exact peak be derived rather than presumed. No extra scientific cap is needed.

## Minor M2 — confine cached source digests to the collector that derived them

**Evidence:** proposal lines 62–76,109; `study_reference/document/execution_descriptors.rs:495–500,501–523,525–591,593–612`.

The existing source helpers derive their parameter/bootstrap hashes from their own facts. The proposed `_bytes_with` variants accept borrowed digest strings, so their correctness now depends on the caller pairing hashes with those same facts. Fix that trust boundary in the source brief: keep construction/use of the cache inside the fixed same-owner collector (or bind a private cache to the exact owner), and keep size-only placeholders inaccessible to output methods. Existing no-cache source helpers must continue deriving hashes from their actual source. A generic crate-visible bag of arbitrary digest strings must not become a source-validation shortcut. This is a confinement requirement, not a request to rehash every field or add an identity framework.

## Accepted design aspects and scope interpretation

- The old-P2/new-retention distinction is explicit and compatible with retaining existing ReceiptLimits semantics. `study_admission.rs:65–69` excludes transient P2 allocations; `describe_candidate` (`1258–1290`), `make_stages` (`1128–1193`) and manifest construction (`1194–1254`) really allocate before the proposed retention handoff. Reserving before moving already-created geometry is honest **retention accounting**, not a pre-allocation bound on that earlier P2 work. This review does not silently extend M1 to every old P2 allocation, nor approve a claim that those allocations are now bounded. The proposal's conditional wording should become that explicit source-brief boundary; any broader all-preparation bound requires its separately identified sizing work.
- Moving geometry and the block training union is feasible because CandidateDescription owns those fields (`study_admission.rs:419–426`). The source-owned geometry helper can see the private identity vectors and complete nested geometry (`ckks-train-nn/src/diagonal_plan.rs:149–156,244–251`). Counting nested requested payload once, retaining the moved geometry outer buffer until its owning iterator drops, and separately charging Description/activation/transpose/class storage avoids the current cloning without changing P3 bytes. Check all borrowed constraints before consuming the diagnostic-bearing candidate.
- One accounting owner seeded with the unchanged plan charge, checked reservation, temporary-to-retained transfer, and actual-scope release is a coherent mechanism. Plan/P1/context reuse does not require a payload copy. New output vectors remain individually subject to A_MAX; the original request's max_bytes/max_records remain unchanged. Error diagnostics are explicitly outside the successful-output zero-allocation claim.
- Shared encoder traversal for size and output, one retained I parameter buffer, borrowed NI P1 bytes, and one cached set of parameter/bootstrap digests eliminate the current repeated temporary encoding without inventing parameters or hash cycles. Security/source checks and frozen P1/P3/P/R/new-domain bytes must survive factoring. Counting real identity and transcript traversals is necessary: current `derive_identities` actually creates both byte buffers and digests; a constant multiplier would not be a substitute.
- The six proposed canonical positives have a coherent conditional work inventory: for I/NI at S=1,2,3 and U=2, two geometry/initial passes yield 24 geometry and 24 initial calls, while planning contributes 24 updates, 12 handoffs and six wrapper calls. Planning plus preparation gives 12 canonical binding executions. Additional sizing traversals, validation passes, refusals and construction calls must be named separately in the eventual source/runtime inventory; these are not measured counts or proof that all six cases already succeed. Three shared materializations, two secure parameter owners and one NI metadata owner are proposed fixture ownership, not observed construction results.
- The actual `StudyRunRequest` lacks planning/request fields (`study_runtime.rs:33–37`), and `run_owned` currently ends at `MissingExecutionDependency` (`308–348`). Keeping this package disconnected there is correct. Explicit immutable consumption/request wiring and the fixed CPU continuation belong to the next reviewed integration. Full-only registration with Quick consumption is operational test coverage, not Full scientific admission. Genuine gates, durable start, post-start binding checks and lease/drop ordering remain required.

## Findings

### I1 — Important: the common scope and input/evidence contracts still require a full target for Quick

Evidence: proposal lines 66, 78, 84, 86 versus lines 132–136; `docs/study-target-design.md:469–481` and `:532–546`.

The proposed single immutable `AdmissionScope` requires lock/reference links, lock ID and threshold bits. `check_inputs` compares against the target's independently materialized receipt. The common evidence prefix likewise requires canonical CaseIdentity and exact lock/reference links, while the consuming checked-case constructor requires identical AdmissionScope across all five capabilities. The separate Quick entry explicitly accepts no VerifiedTargetForSeed, has no full admission/start/threshold, and nevertheless uses these same five constructors. The frozen CaseIdentity encodes only `scope:full=0`; adding Quick to that encoding is expressly excluded.

The intent that Quick has a sealed operational identity is clear, but it does not define how these mandatory full-only checks/fields are absent without weakening the Full constructor. An implementer currently must invent placeholders, broadly optional target fields, or a second implicit path around the stated common checks. This is a capability/scope contract gap, not a request for Quick to acquire a full target.

**Required resolution:** specify a closed Full/Quick scope distinction at the shared constructor boundary (an enum or an equally concrete private design is sufficient). Full must continue to require its verified target/reference/threshold links and independently materialized target comparison. Quick must bind its full registration as registration provenance plus a separate operational Quick identity, exact seed0/two-update consumption and attempt identity; it must perform canonical source/materialization validation without claiming a target-receipt comparison. State which input check and evidence fields are common and which are branch-specific. The evidence identity must distinguish branches without changing frozen full CaseIdentity, ExecutionBinding or admission/start bytes. Quick capabilities must be consumable only by the private Quick entry, with no promotion into CheckedCaseForAdmission/StartedHeCase. Keep the same five substantive gates and their live rechecks; do not add a sixth gate or caller-controlled bypass flag.

Before the relevant source package, freeze branch tests: Full with missing target rejects; Quick requires no target and cannot satisfy full admission; target/threshold placeholders cannot satisfy Full; equal registration/input hashes do not allow a Quick capability to be consumed as Full; actual Quick consumption remains bound. This asks for design/source acceptance criteria, not test execution now.

### M1 — Minor: evidence-sidecar custody must be part of the closed protocol inventory before source

Evidence: proposal lines 86–88, 94–102, 143–144; `docs/study-target-design.md:553–578`, `:601–647`.

The new per-gate records are to be retained alongside the attempt, while their fixed storage names and held-file policy are deferred. That deferral is explicitly acknowledged and is acceptable for this design stage, but it must cover transaction behavior as well as names/tags/caps. The existing store is a closed Target/Admission/Start publication surface; Initial eligibility rejects any named attempt, including empty or orphan attempts. Writing a sidecar before the guarded eligibility decision or treating a partial sidecar as ignorable could change those semantics even with unchanged admission bytes.

**Required before protocol/gate source:** specify the closed storage location/names, allowed entries and caps, when evidence bytes become durable relative to admission, guard/held-FD/no-clobber/fsync ownership, and recovery/Initial/Retry treatment of every partially published evidence state. Require the admission's evidence hash to identify the exact retained bytes. Preserve unknown-entry rejection and the rule that partial/orphan evidence never resets Initial eligibility. This can be a narrow closed extension; it does not require a generic publisher or a new admission payload field.

## Accepted decisions and concrete source acceptance boundaries

1. **Prerequisites, construction obligations and outcomes.** Proposal lines 38–58 preserve the original KAT/security/noninteractive-correctness obligation. Applicable existing correctness/security evidence and declared registered policy remain prerequisites; checking the actual keys, encryption metadata and affected operations remains a construction/use obligation. A failed obligation cannot produce a valid scientific row. RQ1 accuracy, latency, key-switch ratio and time-to-target are measured outcomes, so requiring the new row's successful outcome before its own start would be circular. No new numerical tolerance or unknown policy threshold is introduced. The classification must become an exact applicability/obligation inventory before positive production constructors; it does not itself supply missing secure-N evidence. A failed performance target may be reported honestly; failed correctness is not upgraded into a valid row. These are existing evidence requirements, not a new permission workflow.

2. **Prefer the inert context-instance approach, with the stated limits.** Proposal line 68 is feasible with the current input API. `crates/ckks-train-bench/src/study_provenance/context.rs:953–979` checks the materialization and rechecks exact context identity; `:1014–1029` shows that CheckedMaterializedInput owns facts and borrows the context, not the materialization. A pre-admission gate can retain inert instance identity and owned checked facts without retaining that context-borrowing token for `'p`. Admission must compare the original instance/ProcessStamp, retain the actual `'c` context, obtain the context-scoped checked-input token afresh, compare its facts, and retain it through the live frame. Make this promotion explicit in the source brief. A new metadata-equal verified context must receive a different instance identity; allocation is process-wide, checked, never reused/wrapped, and private. The identity is not serializable authority, a dereferenceable pointer, or a replacement for current process/runtime/pool/evidence rechecks. Preserve pointer equality between Train artifact context and store context. No `'c:'p` relationship or unsafe lifetime extension is needed; do not retain a single-lifetime capture that silently reintroduces it. Compile/negative identity checks remain future source verification, not observations from this review.

3. **Exactly five genuine capabilities and durable start.** Proposal lines 74–120 preserve the ordered five Gates fields while distinguishing evidence records from live capability owners. CheckedCase consumes the genuine capabilities; parsing, recovery, a digest or a fixture cannot mint one. The guarded durable admission/start and the same exclusive store borrow/held handles continue through begin revalidation. Actual setup follows durable start, with accounted post-start validate_plan_binding against the retained request/parameters and no repeated candidate preparation. Setup failure consumes the attempt rather than retrospectively fabricating start or resetting eligibility. This is consistent with `docs/study-execution-design.md:87–119` and the target lifecycle. The eventual integration must actually reach these transitions; disconnected CPU bodies do not establish them.

4. **Ownership, accounting and evidence limits.** The proposed driver retains the exact plan/parameter/preparation/descriptor owners and transfers accounting into the live frame before crypto construction. Crypto must drop before resource/user leases and retained accounting, including unused-driver drop and setup failure, as already required by `docs/study-execution-design.md:9`, `:105–119`, `:134–136`. Post-success P5 snapshot rules remain unchanged. PreparationAccounting measures requested payload, not physical admission or RSS. Proposal lines 124–128 correctly require a separate complete physical-liveness model, actual host/cgroup observations and a held cooperative claim, with no claim that the claim reserves RAM. Its source specification must define the accounting baseline so already resident owners and future incremental allocations are not accidentally compared twice against current free capacity. Missing terms/applicability fail closed; arbitrary headroom constants or a declaration-only model cannot fill them. Existing user authority is recorded and rechecked; CLI selectors cannot enlarge it and an extra user confirmation is not implied.

5. **Frozen contracts and work scope.** The proposal retains existing encoding domains/bytes and caps, independent context/input lifetimes and the original request budgets. It preserves full 21 profiles / 2,680 family rows + 20 exact-Iris rows + seven supplement entries and 268 family Quick projections, full encrypted-block holds and unresolved supplement/deferred Task53 scope. The ordinary CPU branch is an initial implementation package, not Task54 completion. The prospective full publisher/reference lineage and actual external/GPU provenance remain separate concrete work. The proposed source/test inventories distinguish synthetic ordering controls, filesystem/native evidence and real constructors; exact constructor counts, vectors and fault inventories must be frozen before their respective execution, not inferred from this design review.
